§ 07 · Questions

What buyers ask us first.

What am I paying for?

The software licence, every update while you subscribe, and support. Your AI usage is billed by your provider directly, and there is none in offline mode.

Do I need an AI key?

Either a key from DeepSeek, OpenAI or Claude, or local models on your own hardware on the Enterprise plan. You can switch at any time.

Where do my documents go?

They stay on the machine you install it on. With a cloud key, the question and the matching passages are sent to that provider to write the answer. Offline, nothing leaves.

What happens if I stop paying?

Your installation keeps working for 14 days, then the widget pauses. Your data stays on your machine, and the console still opens so you can export it.

What does it run on?

Windows 10 or 11 with the installer, or any server that runs Docker. The public widget needs that machine to be reachable, or a secure tunnel.

Can you host it for me?

Yes. Managed hosting is an add-on: we run your installation on our server with a public address.

Does it work with WordPress?

Yes. There is a WordPress plugin, and a single script tag for any other site.
