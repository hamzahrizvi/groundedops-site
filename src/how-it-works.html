§ 01 · How it works

Three steps, and two places it stops.

What you just clicked through is the whole pipeline. Most assistants only have the middle step.

Step 1

Upload your manuals

PDF, Word or text, read page by page so every answer can point back to where it came from.

Step 2 · first stop

Search, rank, and gate

If nothing relevant turns up, it refuses before writing a word. If two answers fit, it asks which.

Step 3 · second stop

Check, then show

Every draft is checked against its source pages. A draft that fails never reaches your customer.

§ 03 · Your models, your bill

You pay us for the software. Never for your AI usage.

Bring your own key

DeepSeek, OpenAI or Claude, billed to you by the provider. The question and the matching manual passages go to them to write the answer.

Fully offline

Local models on your own hardware. Nothing leaves the building. Answers are slower and simpler than the big cloud models.

No surprise bills

Daily caps per visitor, approved answers that cost nothing to serve, and an email before your provider credit runs out.
