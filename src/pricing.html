Licence questions

What happens if a payment fails?

Your installation keeps working for 14 days while you update your card. Then the widget pauses until the licence is renewed.

Does it need the internet?

It checks its licence now and then. Offline sites can activate with a licence file instead.

Can I move it to a new server?

Yes. Release the old installation in your account and activate the new one with the same key.

What if I cancel?

It runs until the end of the period you paid for. Your data is on your machine, so there’s nothing to hand back.

Do trials include everything?

Yes, Professional features for 14 days. Pick a plan at any point to keep going.

Can I pay by invoice?

Yes, on annual Professional and Enterprise plans.
