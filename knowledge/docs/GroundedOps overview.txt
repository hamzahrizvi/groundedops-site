GroundedOps overview

What GroundedOps is
GroundedOps is a support assistant that answers questions from your own product manuals and technical documents. You install it on your own machine, load your manuals into it, and put a chat widget on your website. Your customers ask questions in their own words and get answers taken from your documents, each one citing the manual and the exact page it came from, with the original document one click away.

What makes it different
Most chatbots always produce an answer, even when they do not know. GroundedOps is built the other way round. Every answer is checked against the pages it came from before it is shown. If the documents do not back the answer up, the answer is not shown: the assistant says it does not know and offers the visitor a route to a person, such as emailing your support team. If a question could mean two different things, it asks which one the visitor means instead of guessing.

The tagline is: Grounded, or it says nothing.

Who it is for
GroundedOps is built for companies whose products come with long technical manuals: spec tables, part numbers, fault codes, installation procedures and troubleshooting steps. Typical buyers are support and customer experience teams who answer the same "it's in the manual" questions every day, documentation teams who want to know where their manuals are weak, and IT and security teams who need documents to stay on their own hardware.

Answering features
GroundedOps answers only from your own documents, with page-level citations. Every generated answer goes through a grounding check, and answers that fail it are never shown. When it cannot answer, it refuses honestly and routes the visitor to a person. When a question is ambiguous, it asks a clarifying question. Step-by-step procedures are kept in order, copied from the manual rather than rewritten. Cross-product questions, such as "which of your products run on 24 V?", are answered from your catalogue and spec tables without generating text.

Improving over time
The curated FAQ lets your team approve answers to common questions once; approved answers are served instantly, word for word, with no AI call. The gaps report logs every question the assistant could not answer, so you know what to add to your documentation next.

Website, models and accounts
The website widget works on any site with one script tag, and there is a WordPress plugin. You choose the AI model: your own DeepSeek, OpenAI or Claude key, or local models that run fully offline. Admin accounts have Owner, Admin and Viewer roles, and people can sign in with Microsoft or Google work accounts.

How it is sold
GroundedOps is sold as a self-hosted licence with a monthly or annual fee. The licence covers the software, every update while you subscribe, and support. Your AI usage is billed by your AI provider directly, or there is none at all if you use offline local models. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial with no card needed.
