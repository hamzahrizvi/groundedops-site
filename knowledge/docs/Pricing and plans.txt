Pricing and plans

Note: this website is a staging preview. The prices below are the current proposed prices and may change before launch.

How pricing works
You pay GroundedOps a fixed licence fee for the software, every update while you subscribe, and support. The fee does not change with how many questions your visitors ask. Your AI usage is separate: if you use your own DeepSeek, OpenAI or Claude key, that provider bills you directly for the answers it writes. Approved FAQ answers and refusals make no AI call at all. If you use offline local models, there is no AI bill.

Free trial
Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial. No card is needed. You receive a trial licence key by email and download the installer straight away. The trial includes Professional features. After 14 days the widget pauses until you choose a plan; nothing is charged automatically.

The Starter plan
Starter costs 199 US dollars per month, or 1,990 dollars per year. It includes 1 installation, 1 website with the widget, up to 15 products in your catalogue, 2 admin seats, all updates while you subscribe, and email support.

The Professional plan
Professional costs 599 US dollars per month, or 5,990 dollars per year. It includes 2 installations, for example production and a staging copy for testing changes, 3 websites with the widget, unlimited products, 10 admin seats, sign-in with Microsoft or Google (SSO), the option to remove "Powered by GroundedOps" from the widget, all updates while you subscribe, and priority support.

The Enterprise plan
Enterprise starts at 18,000 US dollars per year and is quoted after a conversation about your deployment. It includes unlimited installations and websites, offline local models set up with you, unlimited admin seats with SSO, a named support contact with agreed response times, and custom licence terms.

Websites per plan
Starter allows the widget on 1 website, Professional on 3 websites, and Enterprise on as many websites as you need. If you need more websites than Professional allows, Enterprise is the plan for that.

Annual billing
Paying annually costs the same as 10 months, so you get 2 months free.

Discounts
The standard discount is annual billing, which gives you 2 months free. For larger deployments, several sites, or other discount questions, talk to the GroundedOps team.

Add-ons
Setup and onboarding is a one-off service costing 1,500 to 5,000 dollars, quoted after a short call: we load your manuals, build your catalogue and approve your first FAQ answers with you. Managed hosting costs 149 dollars per month: we run your installation on our server with a public address, so the website widget is always reachable. Extra support hours cost 120 dollars per hour, for custom integration, tuning for your documents, or training your team.

Features included in every plan
Grounding checks, page citations, clarifying questions, honest refusals, the curated FAQ, the gaps report, and the choice of your own DeepSeek, OpenAI or Claude key are included in every plan.

Paying by invoice
Invoice payment is available on annual Professional and Enterprise plans.
